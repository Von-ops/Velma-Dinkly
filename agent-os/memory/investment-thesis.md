# 📈 Investment Thesis — Trading Wiz

## Strategy Overview

### Primary: LEAPS (Long-term Equity AnticiPation Securities)
- **What:** Deep-in-the-money call options, 12-24 months out
- **Why:** Stock replacement strategy — control 100 shares for a fraction of cost, unlimited upside, defined risk
- **Ideal candidates:** High-conviction growth stocks (EYL primary target per InvestAnswers)
- **Selection criteria:** Delta > 0.80, 12-24 months to expiration, low IV rank for purchase

### Secondary: Income Generation
- **Iron Condors:** Sell OTM call spread + sell OTM put spread, collect premium
  - Target: $1-$3 per contract, 30-45 DTE, manage at 50% profit or 21 DTE
  - Weekly income: $100-$300/week when deployed (scaling as capital grows)
- **Credit Spreads:** Bull put spreads on pullbacks, bear call spreads on rallies
  - Same risk management: defined max loss, collect time decay

### Tertiary: Diagonal Calendar Spreads
- Sell short-term options against long-term LEAPS positions
- Generate income on existing positions while maintaining upside exposure
- More advanced — deploy after 6 months of iron condor practice

## Investment Thesis: EYL (Current Focus)
- **Source:** InvestAnswers (James Altucher) methodology
- **Thesis:** EYL has asymmetric upside potential
- **Research:** Track quarterly earnings, revenue growth, margin expansion
- **Entry:** Wait for pullback to support, buy LEAPS calls at optimal strike
- **Position sizing:** Max 2% of trading capital at risk per position
- **Exit:** Target 50-100% return, or hold to 6 months from expiration for roll

## Risk Management Rules
1. **Never risk more than 2%** of total trading capital on any single trade
2. **Maintain 6-month emergency fund** — trading capital is separate from life savings
3. **Paper trade for 30 days minimum** before going live
4. **All trades documented** in trade log (see templates/)
5. **Weekly review** of all open positions — no "set and forget"
6. **Stop loss on LEAPS:** 50% loss on premium = exit, reassess thesis
7. **Max portfolio concentration:** No more than 20% in a single underlying
