# 🎨 Brand Kit — Von Empire

## Color Palette
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Deep Black | `#050508` | Backgrounds, primary surfaces |
| Vibrant Gold | `#FFD700` | Accents, highlights, CTAs |
| Vibranium Purple | `#7B2D8E` | Secondary accents, glow elements |
| Rich Green | `#10b981` | Success states, growth metrics |
| Electric Blue | `#3b82f6` | Links, interactive elements |
| Slate Text | `#e2e8f0` | Primary text |
| Muted | `#64748b` | Secondary text, labels |

## Typography
- **Headings:** Modern, bold, tight letter-spacing (Orbitron/Inter feel)
- **Body:** Clean, readable (Inter/Source Sans Pro)
- **Numbers/Finance:** Monospace or tabular nums

## Voice & Tone
- **Direct and authoritative** — Von doesn't hedge. He states.
- **Data-driven** — Always back claims with numbers
- **Story-rich** — The immigrant-to-owner journey is the engine
- **Confident but not arrogant** — "Here's what I did, here's how you can too"
- **Conversational** — Writes like he talks (which he does with chairside karaoke 😄)
- **Never salesy** — Teaches first, sells second

## Taglines & Hooks
- "The $812,000 mistake most new dentists don't see coming"
- "Dental is the foundation, not the ceiling"
- "From Trinidad to 5 practices — here's the playbook"
- "Build wealth WHILE you practice, not after"

## Logo Direction
- Crown element (QUE — "Von Empire")
- Tooth + crown combination
- Minimal, premium, dark theme
- Gold on black

## Content Pillars (Dr. Dentrepreneur)
1. **Money** — Student debt, compensation models, practice economics
2. **Career Path** — Associate → ownership → DSO/exit strategies
3. **Mindset** — Immigrant hustle, building beyond the chair
4. **Systems** — How Von runs 5 locations, processes, tech
5. **Life** — Dad journey, marriage, tennis, travel (keep it real)
