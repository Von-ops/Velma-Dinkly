# 📱 Instagram Post Template

## Format: Reel/Short-form Video Script

### Hook (First 3 seconds — MUST grab attention)
- [ ] Number/Statistic hook: "Here's why $812,000 matters..."
- [ ] Contrarian hook: "Everyone tells you to buy a practice. Here's why that's wrong."
- [ ] Personal hook: "I moved here from Trinidad with $800 and a dental degree..."
- [ ] Question hook: "You're making $180K as an associate. Why do you feel broke?"

### Body (30-60 seconds)
**Story:**
[Insert personal story, case study, or data-driven example]
- Where it started
- What changed
- The result

**Data Point:**
[One compelling number that proves the point]
- "The average dental associate who doesn't invest loses $X over 10 years"

**Lesson/Takeaway:**
[Actionable advice — the "do this next" moment]
- Step 1
- Step 2
- Why it works

### CTA (Last 5 seconds)
- [ ] "Follow @DrDentrepreneur for the rest"
- [ ] "Save this before it gets buried"
- [ ] "DM me 'ROADMAP' — I'll send you my playbook"
- [ ] "Comment 'OWNERSHIP' if you want part 2"

```

```

## Format: Carousel Post
Same structure, but:
- Slide 1: Hook headline
- Slides 2-8: Teaching points (one per slide)
- Slide 9: Summary
- Slide 10: CTA + save prompt
