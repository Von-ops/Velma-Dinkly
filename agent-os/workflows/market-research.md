# 🔍 Workflow: Market Research — Trading Wiz

## Phase 1: Universe Screening
1. Use InvestAnswers picks + personal watchlist
2. Filter for: market cap > $5B, sector diversification, clean growth story
3. Exclude: meme stocks, highly volatile, earnings this week

## Phase 2: LEAPS Apply Filters
- Delta ≥ 0.80 (mimics stock movement)
- DTE: 12-24 months
- IV Rank: < 50 (buying cheap premium)
- Open Interest: > 100 (liquidity)
- Bid-Ask spread: < $0.50

## Phase 3: Score Candidates
Score 1-5 on each dimension:
- Fundamental thesis (1-5): Revenue growth, margins, market position
- Risk/reward (1-5): % upside potential vs max loss
- IV attractiveness (1-5): Low IV = better entry
- Sector momentum (1-5): Is the sector trending up?

## Phase 4: Risk Assessment
- Max loss if stock stays flat: premium paid
- Max loss if stock drops 30%: premium paid (or ~30% of stock equivalent)
- Max gain if stock rises 50%: ~40-60x leverage effect on premium
- Position size: max 2% of capital at risk

## Phase 5: Plan
- Entry: specific price level to buy at
- Stop: 50% premium loss
- Target: 50-100% premium profit
- Roll plan: at 6 months DTE, evaluate roll up or keep

## Phase 6: Execute & Document
- Execute trade (paper or live)
- Log in outputs/trading-wiz/trade-log.md
- Set alert for entry/stop/target levels
