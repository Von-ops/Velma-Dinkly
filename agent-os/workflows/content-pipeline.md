# 🎬 Workflow: Content Pipeline — Dr. Dentrepreneur

## Overview
Weekly content production system. 3 Reels/posts + 1 long-form + 1 newsletter. Batch mode = Von records 2 hours, Von gets 1 month of content.

## Step 1: Research & Ideation (Monday)
- Scan dental news, Reddit r/dentistry, student debt articles
- Pull 5 hook ideas from Von's notes or real conversations
- Check trending dental topics on IG/TikTok
- Map to content pillars: Money, Career, Mindset, Systems, Life

## Step 2: Script (Monday)
For each piece of content, write:
```
Hook (first 3 seconds): "The number that matters..."
Body (30-60 sec): Story + data point + lesson
CTA (last 5 sec): "Follow @DrDentrepreneur for more" or "DM me 'ROADMAP'"
```

## Step 3: Record (Tuesday — batch day)
- Block 2 hours
- Film 10+ short videos in one sitting
- Use ring light, phone on tripod, clean background
- Multiple takes per video (best of 3)

## Step 4: Edit (Wednesday)
- CapCut: add captions, trim silence, add B-roll
- Export in 9:16 vertical
- Save drafts to phone

## Step 5: Schedule (Thursday)
- Post to IG Reels: Mon, Wed, Fri at 9 AM EST
- Schedule via Meta Business Suite
- Write caption + hashtags in advance

## Step 6: Engage (Daily, 15 min)
- Reply to all comments within first hour
- DM new followers with a genuine note
- Comment on 5 target accounts' posts

## Step 7: Analyze (Sunday)
- Check IG insights: reach, engagement rate, saves
- Which posts performed best? Why?
- Plan next week based on data
